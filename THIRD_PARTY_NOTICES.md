# sudokUI

The complete `src/vendor/engine` directory is imported unchanged from
https://github.com/AImenes/sudokUI at commit
`2eb9de333e4119e27778a2648e8e29123a3386f9`.

All technique implementations and their catalogue are retained. The sandwich
adapter excludes the classic uniqueness family from automatic deductions because
variant clues invalidate the classic deadly-pattern premise. Upstream unimplemented
entries are labelled as roadmap, and redundant techniques retain upstream defaults.
The variant exact-search solver replaces classic brute force in this application's UI.
